Featured Projects & Research Interests

Selected work and developing ideas at the intersection of engineering, food processing and sustainability.

Agricultural Waste Valorization Using an Immobilized-Enzyme Packed-Bed Reactor

Status: Proposal / idea stage

A proposed technology to convert lignocellulosic agricultural residues, with maize cobs as the selected feedstock, into fermentable sugars through pretreatment and enzymatic hydrolysis using an immobilized-enzyme packed-bed reactor.

The planned work includes reactor concept development, kinetic modelling, scale-up assessment and techno-economic analysis. Experimental performance and costs will need to be established through testing.

Assessment of Factors Influencing Flour Extraction Rate

Context: Industrial wheat milling

A process-improvement study examining variation in flour extraction rate and its possible relationship with wheat moisture content and operating conditions in an industrial milling plant.

The work emphasizes production data collection, process monitoring, statistical analysis and identifying opportunities to improve consistency.
